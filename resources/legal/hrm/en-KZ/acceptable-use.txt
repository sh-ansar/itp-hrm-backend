ACCEPTABLE USE RULES — ITP HRM
Draft for legal review. Jurisdiction: Kazakhstan.

1. Intended use
1.1. ITP HRM is intended for legitimate personnel administration, including employees, staffing, leave, attendance and HR documents.
1.2. Every user must act within their authorized organizational and job responsibilities. Security roles and audit mechanisms apply regardless of tariff.

2. Prohibited behaviour
2.1. Accessing another organization or employee's private records without permission, password attacks, impersonation and unauthorized changes to privileges.
2.2. Sharing account credentials, bypassing server-side permissions or circumventing any enabled subscription restrictions.
2.3. Importing or disclosing identity, medical, compensation or other employee personal data without an appropriate lawful purpose and necessary access restriction.
2.4. Bulk unauthorized extraction, sale or publication of employee records.
2.5. Falsifying personnel orders, payroll, staffing history, leave balances, timesheets or audit records.
2.6. Uploading malware, probing infrastructure without authorization, bot registrations, deliberate excessive traffic and denial-of-service attempts.
2.7. Using HRM for fraudulent, unlawful or unrelated commercial activity.

3. Employer responsibilities
3.1. The employer maintains accurate entries and confirms the legality of each personnel decision and document.
3.2. Permissions must be updated upon employee transfer or departure. Suspected credential compromise must be reported immediately.
3.3. Sensitive health or other private materials must be minimized and available only to properly authorized personnel.

4. Integration and automation
4.1. Automation and APIs may be used only within published, authorized interfaces and rate limits.
4.2. Data exchange between companies requires explicit authorization and must not defeat tenant isolation.
4.3. UI visibility is not itself permission to execute restricted backend operations.

5. Incident handling
5.1. The Provider may apply proportionate protective measures to stop attacks and investigate misuse, subject to applicable law and contractual obligations.
5.2. Employee records cannot be arbitrarily deleted as a punitive measure.
5.3. Report incidents to info@itpmining.kz without exposing other employees' data.

6. Updates
6.1. Changes are versioned and require appropriate review and publication. This is a draft, not current binding rules.
