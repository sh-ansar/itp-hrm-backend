PUBLIC OFFER — ITP HRM SOFTWARE SERVICE
Draft for legal review. Jurisdiction: Republic of Kazakhstan. This English version is not effective unless separately approved and published.

1. Parties and purpose
1.1. ITP Mining LLP (Provider) offers business customers access to ITP HRM at https://platform.itpmining.kz/hrm/.
1.2. The software supports employee records, staffing and organizational structure, personnel operations, leave schedules and balances, timesheets, personnel documents, reports, training and talent reserve, within the modules included in a published plan.
1.3. The Provider supplies software, not employer-of-record services, legal advice or automatic compliance certification. Each Customer remains responsible for its employment decisions, labour records, accuracy and legal obligations.
1.4. A company administrator may manage its own tenant but does not gain platform-wide administrative powers.

2. Registration and acceptance
2.1. A duly authorized representative verifies a business email, reviews the currently published applicable legal documents, and creates an organization.
2.2. Acceptance of a published version and, where applicable, payment of an agreed invoice establishes the contractual relationship to the extent permitted by law.
2.3. Each legal version has a jurisdiction, language, effective date and recorded consent. An unpublished draft is not an offer capable of acceptance.
2.4. The representative may not accept personal-data processing terms on behalf of all employees. The employer must have separate, valid grounds to enter and process their information.

3. HRM functionality
3.1. The published plan controls the contracted scope of functional modules and capacity. Authentication, confidentiality, role-based access restrictions and security audit mechanisms remain mandatory safeguards regardless of plan.
3.2. Employee profiles, employment agreements, medical absence evidence, compensation, performance and career records may include sensitive personal data. The Customer configures appropriate access and ensures legitimate processing.
3.3. The Customer reviews all generated orders, reports and printable forms before legal use. The Provider is not responsible for HR decisions made from incorrect input.

4. Plans, billing and subscriptions
4.1. The currently published plan and Tariff Policy define prices, limits, duration and invoicing terms. Product plans are organization-specific; Portal multi-company entitlements do not transfer to HRM.
4.2. A generated invoice or uploaded proof of payment does not constitute settlement. Paid access requires verification by an authorized Provider representative.
4.3. An unpaid plan may remain pending payment. Technical plan enforcement is only enabled following tested and documented rollout.
4.4. Versioned plan conditions attached to a paid subscription remain recorded and are not retroactively replaced by a newly published draft.

5. Customer obligations
5.1. Enter accurate personnel information, limit access to authorized staff, maintain lawful grounds for processing employee personal data, and immediately revoke obsolete access.
5.2. Protect credentials, report incidents and prevent unauthorized access to other companies.
5.3. Do not upload unlawful material or use the service for purposes inconsistent with employment administration.

6. Provider obligations
6.1. Deliver the contracted functionality and apply reasonable organizational and technical safeguards, including access separation and event logging.
6.2. Handle support requests and communicate material changes to paid terms.
6.3. Do not exploit Customer employee data for unrelated marketing or disclosure outside the contractual and lawful purposes.
6.4. Necessary planned maintenance and incident recovery may cause interruptions; service commitments are governed by the agreed contract.

7. Confidentiality, legal duties and disputes
7.1. Personal-data processing is subject to the Privacy Policy, applicable agreements and Kazakhstan law. The Customer determines the lawful purposes and categories of employee records.
7.2. Processing data from another jurisdiction, including cross-border transfers, requires separate applicable safeguards and legal review.
7.3. The parties remain liable for their own breaches under applicable law. Mandatory statutory rights are not excluded.
7.4. Complaints should first be directed to the Provider and resolved through discussions; unresolved matters follow applicable jurisdiction and contractual provisions.

8. Term, termination and records
8.1. The agreement continues for the accepted and paid period, subject to lawful termination.
8.2. Termination does not authorize unrestricted deletion of employment records subject to statutory retention obligations. Export, preservation and deletion must follow the applicable agreement and law.
8.3. Updated offers are separately versioned and published; they do not silently modify already paid periods.
