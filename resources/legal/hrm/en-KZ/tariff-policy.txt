ITP HRM TARIFF POLICY AND SERVICE TERMS
Draft for commercial and legal approval. Jurisdiction: Kazakhstan.

1. Scope
1.1. The tariff policy governs plans for ITP HRM only, not the ITP Portal, Inventory or MRO applications.
1.2. Only the currently approved and published plan version is a public offer. Draft or internal versions are not available for purchase.
1.3. Each subscription is associated with one HRM organization; a unified multi-company subscription is not currently supported.

2. Proposed plans and HRM features
2.1. Three monthly draft tariffs have been adapted from the existing ITP Portal commercial price reference:
Start — KZT 14,900 per month, 2 licensed user accounts, 50 employee records, one organization; employees, staffing, HR operations, documents, and mandatory permissions.
Growth — KZT 69,900 per month, 5 licensed user accounts, 150 employee records, one organization; adds leave schedules and balances, timesheets, work planning and reports.
Business — KZT 299,900 per month, 10 licensed user accounts, 500 employee records, one organization; adds employee learning, development and talent reserve.
2.2. Proposed technical file limits are 1 GiB per organization and 35 MiB per uploaded file. Such limits shall not be advertised as active controls until actually enforced and tested on the backend.
2.3. An employee record is not the same as a licensed login account; these counters have separate limits.
2.4. Authentication, role checks, access logs and privacy safeguards are security controls, never optional paid upgrades.
2.5. Project feeds, chat, calls, project boards and other Portal-only modules do not form part of an HRM plan.

3. Price, tax and invoice
3.1. The above prices are commercial proposals until formally approved. Any applicable tax is specified in the issued invoice according to the Provider's current tax position and applicable law.
3.2. An invoice must specify the selected plan version, period, currency and payment details.
3.3. Invoice creation and uploading a payment receipt do not activate the paid period. Access begins only following verified payment or another documented authorized arrangement.
3.4. No automatic recurring debit is assumed. Renewal and any discounts must be explicitly agreed.
3.5. New prices and module changes cannot retroactively reduce already paid contractual terms.

4. Usage limits and storage
4.1. The plan displays permitted user accounts, employee profiles, organizations, storage and maximum file size separately.
4.2. When technically supported, reaching a plan limit may restrict new operations but does not justify uncontrolled deletion of saved HR records.
4.3. The Customer is responsible for the lawfulness and content of all files and personnel records uploaded to HRM.

5. Additional services
5.1. Data migration, custom development, integration with accounting systems and extended support require an explicit agreement unless listed in an approved plan.
5.2. Features that exist only as designs are not included until implemented and accepted.

6. Complaints and refunds
6.1. Claims about incorrect billing, duplicate payments or service disruptions are handled in accordance with the contract and mandatory applicable law.
6.2. Send billing questions with the invoice reference to info@itpmining.kz. Never email passwords, CVV codes or complete payment-card data.
6.3. Legal review must confirm refund conditions, taxation, effective date and the complete payment workflow before this policy may be published.

7. Changes
7.1. Tariff changes require a new version, jurisdiction, effective date and authorized publication.
7.2. This document remains a draft until commercial, technical and legal approval.
