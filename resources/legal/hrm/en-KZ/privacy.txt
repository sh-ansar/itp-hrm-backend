ITP HRM PRIVACY POLICY
Draft for legal review. Jurisdiction: Republic of Kazakhstan.

1. Scope and roles
1.1. ITP Mining LLP provides the ITP HRM technical service for employee administration. The employer organization decides why and on what lawful basis its employees' information is entered and processed.
1.2. The Provider processes account and service information for authentication, contractual service delivery and safety, and handles employee records within the Customer's lawful instructions and applicable obligations.
1.3. This policy is not a blanket employee consent and does not replace separate legal grounds for employee records or cross-border transfers.

2. Data categories
2.1. User information: name, email, role, employer, account settings, photo, protected password hash and session data.
2.2. HR records: employee identity and contacts, identifiers where lawfully collected, department, position, manager, job history, employment documents and personnel orders.
2.3. Work information: payroll and compensation fields, leave balances and schedules, attendance, timesheets, training, career development and talent reserve.
2.4. Sensitive data: medical absence evidence or related health information may require a stricter lawful basis and minimum-necessary processing.
2.5. Technical information: IP address, browser, operating system, sign-in events, essential cookies, audit logs and security diagnostics.
2.6. Billing records: organization details, plan, invoice and payment proof only where commercial features are available.

3. Processing purposes
3.1. Verify users and manage secure, role-limited access to HRM.
3.2. Provide contracted HR operations and support the Customer's legitimate personnel administration.
3.3. Resolve support tickets, preserve integrity and security, and meet applicable contractual or legal requirements.
3.4. Use minimized diagnostics for service quality. Employee records must not be repurposed for unauthorized advertising.

4. Access and security
4.1. The Customer assigns roles according to legitimate business need and removes access when a person leaves or changes duties.
4.2. The Provider uses reasonable access, transmission and audit safeguards. The existence of a file link alone does not grant the right to share that file.
4.3. Security incidents are handled under applicable obligations and the relevant customer agreement.

5. Disclosure and transfers
5.1. Information is shared with authorized Customer personnel, lawfully appointed technical service providers and public authorities only where applicable legal grounds exist.
5.2. Cross-border hosting and transfers require independent verification under relevant laws. An administrator's consent is not employee consent for such transfers.

6. Retention and deletion
6.1. HR retention depends on statutory employer obligations, lawful processing grounds and contractual arrangements.
6.2. Ending a subscription does not automatically authorize permanent deletion of employment records. Export, secure retention and deletion follow agreed procedures and the law.
6.3. Security logs and billing records may be kept for mandatory periods independently of account termination.

7. Cookies and user rights
7.1. Essential cookies enable secure login; selected UI preferences may be stored locally. Disabling required cookies may prevent authentication.
7.2. A data subject may request access, correction and other rights available under applicable law. The employer is normally the first contact for employee HR records.
7.3. Questions addressed to the Provider may be sent to info@itpmining.kz, subject to identity and authorization checks.

8. Effective publication
8.1. This translation and its Kazakhstan-specific legal content require independent review and approved publication. An unpublished draft does not establish current terms.
